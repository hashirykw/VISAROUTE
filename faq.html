Frequently asked questions

Can’t find your answer? Message us and we reply within the hour.

How long does a visa take?

It depends on the country. Each destination page shows typical processing time, from 24 hours for e-visas to several weeks for embassy visas.

Do you guarantee approval?

No agency can. The embassy makes the final decision, but our document review greatly reduces the chance of refusal.

What if my visa is refused?

Government fees are not refundable once submitted. Our service fee is partly refunded as stated in the Refund Policy.

Which payment methods do you accept?

PayPal, credit and debit cards, bank transfer, and mobile wallets such as JazzCash and Easypaisa.

Is my passport data safe?

Documents are encrypted in storage and deleted after your application is processed.

Can I apply for my whole family?

Yes. Choose the number of travellers on the application page and upload everyone’s documents.
