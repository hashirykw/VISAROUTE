Umrah questions

Requirements are set by the Saudi authorities and can change, so we confirm current rules for you.

What documents do I need?

Usually a passport valid for six months or more, photos, and any health or vaccination documents required at the time. Women travelling may have extra requirements. We confirm the latest rules before you pay.

Can I book only the ticket?

Yes. Choose “Umrah ticket” in the form above and we will send fares for Jeddah or Madinah.

Can I go with family or a group?

Yes. We arrange family rooms and group departures with a dedicated guide.

How far in advance should I book?

We suggest at least four to six weeks, and earlier for Ramadan and school holidays.
